hddncharacters

hi! i have spent some time building tools for InDesign that feel intuitive and easy to use, automating aspects of typesetting and the aesthetic principles of print.

they are not meant as a one-size-fits-all solution, but as a time-saver and a solid starting point to build on.

this is an ongoing project, and i decided to share these scripts for free. feedback or ideas for new scripts are always welcome via (@) instagram dm.

(Nr.01) VERSION 1.0

Ragged Right

Evens out the rag of flush-left text within a ragged zone you define. With rag control and optical margins.

() DOWNLOAD

(Nr.02) VERSION 1.0

Centered Ragged Text

Balances the line lengths of centered text into a calm, symmetrical shape. Based on the Ragged Right tool ( Nr.01).

() DOWNLOAD

(Nr.03) VERSION 1.0

Justified Text

Even word spacing and a clean grey value for justified text, with your choice of hyphenation and last-line alignment.

() DOWNLOAD

(Nr.04) VERSION 1.0

Image Fore-Edge

Slices an image into thin strips along the edge of every page, so it appears on the fore-edge of the closed book.

() DOWNLOAD
